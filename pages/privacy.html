Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

To deliver accurate astrological readings and premium services, we may collect the following:

  • Astrological Data: Date, exact time, and geographic location of your birth. This data is strictly used for mathematical calculations to generate your natal charts and celestial readings.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and interaction metrics collected automatically via cookies.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected information to:

  • Maintain your session and preferences (e.g., Personal Grimoire log).
  • Improve our website functionality and user experience.

3. Third-Party Payment Protection

All financial transactions are processed through verified, secure third-party payment gateways (such as Stripe or PayPal). Astro Insight does not collect, store, or process your raw credit card numbers or banking details on our servers.

4. Cookies, Analytics & Advertising (Google Consent Mode v2)

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to analyze site traffic and serve personalized content/advertisements.

  • Consent Management: We employ Google Consent Mode v2. Upon your first visit, you can choose to "Accept All", strictly use "Necessary Only", or "Reject All" non-essential cookies.
  • Analytics & Advertising: If you consent, we use Google Analytics 4 and Google AdSense. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits. We do not use your personal astrological birth data for advertising targeting.
  • Opting Out: You can change your cookie preferences at any time via our website, or opt out of personalized Google advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

5. International Data Transfers

Astro Insight operates globally. Your information, including personal data, may be transferred to, and maintained on, servers located outside of your state, province, or country (including Ukraine and the United States) where the data protection laws may differ from those of your jurisdiction. By using our services, you consent to this transfer.

6. International Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you hold specific digital rights:

  • GDPR (EU/UK Users): You have the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data. You may also object to processing or request data portability.
  • CCPA/CPRA (California Users): You have the right to know what personal data we collect, request its deletion, and opt-out of the sharing of your personal data. (Note: We do not "sell" your personal data to data brokers).

To invoke these rights, please email our support team.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our services are directed at a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EU). If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child without parental consent, we will take steps to remove that information immediately.

8. Data Retention

We retain your astrological and personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. If you request account deletion, your data will be permanently wiped from our active databases within 30 days, except where retention is required for legal or tax accounting purposes.
